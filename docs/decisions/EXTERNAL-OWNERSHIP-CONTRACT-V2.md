# External ownership contract v2

## Decision

Superset BI Agent product `v0.8.0` is the sole BI domain/runtime owner. Its
external contract `2.0.0` exposes typed high-level intents at
`GET /v2/capabilities` and `POST /v2/intents`. ChimpMaera is an optional,
fail-closed client and does not contact Superset or source databases directly.

The capability response is generated by the running SBA process and binds the
actual product version, contract version, accepted Adaptive Graph incumbent,
capabilities and authority boundaries with canonical SHA-256. Every intent
response repeats that runtime identity and has its own canonical digest.

## Intent and authority boundary

The public v2 actions are `status`, `discovery`, `analyze`, `plan`, `preview`
and `readback`. Inputs are closed objects. Free SQL, credentials, raw rows,
arbitrary URLs and direct persistent mutation actions are denied before an
internal control call. Analyze is source-read-only and does not implicitly
publish to Superset.

Persistent Superset change remains an SBA-internal trusted workflow:
preview → exact direct trusted-UI approval → apply → readback → rollback. The
attestation reports those capabilities as non-external intents so a client
cannot mistake their existence for ambient authority. Model output is advisory
and has no mutation authority.

Adaptive Graph v1 remains the accepted incumbent. Partial/negative v2 model or
graph candidates are not promoted or advertised.

## Rollback and nonclaims

Rollback is to SBA `v0.7.1` and the previous CM external-client release. BI is
optional/default-off, so a missing or incompatible SBA service disables only BI
integration. This contract proves local synthetic/fixture compatibility only;
it does not claim production/customer readiness, arbitrary database support,
source-row analysis, free-form dashboard authoring, SSO, HA or multi-tenancy.
